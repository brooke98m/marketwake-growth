Requires a Shift in How We Operate
An offering lead model changes how the company functions at its core. We need to plan for a fully scalable offerings structure from the ground up.
Need a Replicable Playbook
The ultimate opportunity is a model we can take to every Marketwake office we open. A proven playbook that scales with growth.
Creates Hierarchy with Unique Incentives
A proper structure means a full hierarchy of clearly defined roles, supported by unique bonuses and incentives that reward performance at every level.

The root problem

Undefined ownership
is compounding everything.

Undefined ownership is creating compounding gaps across how design work gets scoped, executed, and prioritized. Until we solve ownership, nothing else can be structured around it.

Root Cause
Ownership is undefined. It is unclear who holds final accountability across design work. AM? Platform Specialist? Brooke? Bin? Marley? Or someone else entirely?
Without a defined owner, nothing else can be structured around it.
Missing Effort Framework
There are no templates or benchmarks for how long work takes. Level of effort is unknown, making it impossible to scope, staff, or commit to timelines with confidence.
No Prioritization System
Without a framework, there is no way to distinguish keystone work from everyday tasks. Everything feels equally urgent — which means nothing actually gets treated as a priority.
No Client Hub
There is no central source of up-to-date client information. The team is constantly missing context that should be readily available.

02
Goal of the liaisons

Start with outcomes.
Work backwards.

The liaison role exists to help us build the foundation right. These are the three goals that guide everything we are building toward.

01
Start-to-finish system for clients and employees
A complete, end-to-end system that defines how every client engagement is scoped, sold, onboarded, delivered, and renewed — and how every employee fits within that system with clarity on roles, goals, and growth.
02
Be a technology-first agency
Technology and IP should facilitate every process. We build tools, templates, and systems that make our work repeatable, scalable, and best-in-class — not dependent on any single person's heroics.
03
Start with outcomes, work backwards
Define what the client gets first, then build the process that delivers it. Every offering, every scope, every deliverable should trace back to a clear outcome the client is paying for.
